The studio

We are a two-person team. We design, build, test and run Money Tycoon ourselves, from the economy model to the store pages, and we ship updates on Android and iOS together.

  • Systems first: income, costs and risk follow rules a player can learn and plan around.
  • Fair monetization: rewarded ads and optional purchases, never pay-to-win leaderboards.
  • Players see why things happen, so a bad month is a lesson, not a mystery.
